# Statistical background

This section gives the statistical background for the **q2-classo** and **q2-gglasso** plugins: compositional data analysis, microbiome sequencing count tables and QIIME 2 artifacts, and the transformations used by the two plugin workflows. The remaining subsections describe the mathematical formulations of the implemented estimators in **q2-classo** and **q2-gglasso**.
